# Fake or Not #3: Polymarket "50/50" claims

**Verdict: MISLEADING.** Real wallets and real profits, but the post picks the biggest number, describes a strategy the wallet doesn't follow, and frames one lucky ticket as a strategy.

## Trader A ("+$93.8K buying 41-59¢ contracts, 1.8% of $5.2M")
- The $93,771 matched the profile headline at the time (profile chart: $93,485 on Oct 9; $97,022 headline on Oct 10).
- Polymarket's all-time leaderboard shows $39,652 on $5.33M volume for the same wallet (0.74%).
- Rebuilding every market: $40,029. 2,001 losing sides resolved at $0 but were never redeemed (−$52,382); they don't show in the realized-only numbers.
- The wallet bought both Up and Down in 77% of its 18,587 markets; only 48% of its money went in at 41-59¢; 51.5% of markets ended in profit; ~210 markets a day; $10,772 in taker rebates. That looks like an automated system, not "buy 50/50 and collect $1". The post's own screenshot shows wins bought at 35-37¢.

## Trader B ("one $55 trade = 62%")
- True: 54,999.9 shares at 0.1¢ ($55) on "Bitcoin Up or Down on October 5?" paid +$53,296 = 62.1% of the $85,830 headline (leaderboard $85,004; rebuild $85,029).
- Without it: $30,735 in ~23 months on $8.99M volume (0.34%). Of 964 bets at ≤5¢, 35 were held to a win.

## Paper test of the "50/50 band" (2,160 BTC 5-min markets, Sep 10 → Oct 10, 2026)
- 9 of 10 simple rule variants lost money with 1¢ slippage + taker fee. Coin flip: −7.8% (min 1), −7.1% (min 2).
- The only winner (follow the favorite, min 1: +1.6%) has a 95% interval that includes zero and turns into −8.4% one minute later.
- Break-even at 50¢: 51.75% win rate (fee only), 52.75% with 1¢ slippage. Buying both sides as a taker costs $1.035-$1.055 per $1 payout.

## Context
- Galaxy Research (Oct 1, 2026): 69.2% of 2.9M retail accounts finished below break-even.
- Akey, Grégoire, Harvie & Martineau (working paper, Jun 21, 2026): top 1% of winners capture 76.5% of profits; ~69% of users end with a loss; the top 0.1% do 47.3% of volume as makers vs 17.1% for the bottom 95%.
- The posting account also publishes copy-trading bot results and "free reward" promos; its profile links carry a "via" referral tag.

## What can't be concluded
- Whether Trader A has a real edge going forward. The rebuild says it's profitable (~$40K) but at a much thinner margin than claimed.
- Which of Polymarket's two numbers is "official".
